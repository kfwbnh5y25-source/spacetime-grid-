## Part I — Term-by-Term Audit & Paper Justification of the ODE RHS & Integrator

In [spin-waves/index.html](file:///Users/davidzehner/Work/NMM_TOOL/spacetime-grid-/spin-waves/index.html), the complex modal amplitudes $a_h(\tau) = a_{R,h}(\tau) + j a_{I,h}(\tau)$ ($h = 0,\dots,28$) are evolved by `evalNmmRhs` ([L1966–L2135](file:///Users/davidzehner/Work/NMM_TOOL/spacetime-grid-/spin-waves/index.html#L1966-L2135)) inside the adaptive RK4 + Brown FDT stepper `stepLlg` ([L2137–L2208](file:///Users/davidzehner/Work/NMM_TOOL/spacetime-grid-/spin-waves/index.html#L2137-L2208)):

$$
\frac{da_h}{d\tau} = \frac{1}{1+\alpha^2}\left[ j\bar\omega_h a_h + \mathcal{G}_{1,h}(\tau) - \alpha\sum_{i=0}^{M-1}\!\left(K^+_{hi}a_i - K^-_{hi}a_i^*\right) + \mathcal{G}_{2,h}(\tau,\mathbf{a}) + \mathcal{G}_{3,h}(\mathbf{a}) + \mathcal{G}_{4,h}(\mathbf{a}) \right] + \xi_h(\tau).
$$

Comparing `evalNmmRhs` against **Perna (2022) p. 3, Table 1 & p. 11, Eqs. (46)–(47)**, the solver retains **every exact LLG term present in d'Aquino (2026) p. 4, Eq. (9)** plus the full linear inhomogeneous $\mathbf{m}_0$ forcing/damping terms, and **deliberately omits** the higher-order small corrections below (whose omission is justified in the weakly nonlinear low-damping YIG regime $\alpha \sim 10^{-3}$, $\lvert a_h\rvert \lesssim 0.08$):

1. **Spin-transfer torque ($\beta = 0$)**: Spin-torque terms proportional to $\beta\mathbf{p}$ (**Perna p. 3, Table 1** $\mathbf{f}^c=\mathbf{h}_{\text{rf}}+\alpha\beta\mathbf{p}$, $\mathbf{f}^d=\alpha\mathbf{h}_{\text{rf}}-\beta\mathbf{p}$) are zero because the YIG disk is purely RF-pumped without electric spin-current injection.
2. **Higher-order nonlinear Gilbert damping ($\alpha c^\alpha_{hij}$, $\alpha d^\alpha_{hijk}$)** and **$O(h_{\text{rf}}\lvert a\rvert^2, h_{\text{rf}}\lvert a\rvert^3)$ nonlinear RF scattering ($c^f_{hij}$, $d^f_{hijk}$)** (**Perna p. 3, Table 1 & p. 11, Eqs. (46)–(47)**): For YIG ($\alpha \le 2\times 10^{-3}$) and RF field $h_{\text{rf}}/\mu_0 M_s \lesssim 5\times 10^{-3}$, these enter at relative order $O(\alpha) \sim 10^{-3}$ or $O(h_{\text{rf}}\lvert a\rvert^2) \sim 10^{-5}$ and are omitted in **d'Aquino (2026) p. 4, Sec. II.B, Eq. (9)** ($0 < \alpha \ll 1$).
3. **Quintic and sextic magnon interactions ($e_{hijkl}$, $f_{hijklm}$)** (**Perna p. 2, Eq. (1)** $\tfrac{3}{4!}\delta m_\perp^4$, **p. 3, Table 1**, **p. 11, Eqs. (46)–(47)**): Explicitly truncated at third order in $\mathbf{a}$ ($d_{hijk}$) per **d'Aquino (2026) p. 4, Eq. (9)** (*"here reported as truncated at third-order terms"*), valid well within Perna's **p. 9, Methods §3.2, Eq. (29)** parabolic validity bound ($\delta m_\perp < 0.6$).
4. **Off-diagonal Brown thermal cross-covariances**: Only diagonal noise variances $\propto (K^+_{mm} \mp K^-_{mm})$ are injected, matching diagonal relaxation rates rather than the $29\times 29$ Cholesky decomposition of $\mathbf{K}^\pm$.
